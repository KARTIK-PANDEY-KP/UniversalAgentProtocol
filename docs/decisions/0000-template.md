# NNNN. Title, as a statement of what was decided

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)

## Context

What made this a decision rather than an obvious step. The constraint, the
pressure, or the disagreement. Enough that a reader a year from now understands
the problem without knowing the conversation.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense, as a rule someone could follow.

## Alternatives

What else was on the table and why it was not chosen. This is the part future
readers need most, because a rejected option always looks reasonable when you
cannot see what was wrong with it.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes easy, and what it makes hard. Include the parts
that are inconvenient — a record listing only benefits is a record nobody
trusts.
